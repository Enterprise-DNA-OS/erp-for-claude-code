# Why no front end

A distributor asks what can ship, which supplier is late and which jobs have used their budget. Those answers can come from the records directly. This base puts operational data in Postgres, with a CLI and agent recipes. Read-only HTML shows the week and produces the paperwork.

Screens still help with mobile picking, barcode scans, offline warehouse work, drag-and-drop planning, shared approvals and frequent data entry. Those are not in this free base. Neither are manufacturing planning, accounting, bank feeds or tax filing. Costed dispatch uses the price frozen on the line, not FIFO valuation. The operator checks actual receipts and dispatches before recording them.

Your existing accounting ledger remains the financial system. A local PGlite database serves one process at a time. A shared installation needs Postgres, scoped credentials, backups and controls appropriate to the business. The agent and hosting have their own costs.
